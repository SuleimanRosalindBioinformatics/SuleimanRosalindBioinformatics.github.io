Academic Learning Progression

3-Tier Computational Biology Learning Path

From fundamental DNA transcribing to Hirschberg linear-space alignments, De Bruijn sequence assembly, Baum-Welch HMMs, and 2-break genome rearrangements.

🟢 Beginner (51 Problems) 🟡 Intermediate (196 Problems) 🔴 Advanced (37 Problems)
LEVEL 1 FOUNDATIONS

🟢 Beginner Tier

51 Solved Problems

Foundational molecular biology operations, Python basics, sequence counting, replication origin (OriC) analysis, point mutation metrics, and quality score filtering.

Key Curricular Milestones & Paradigms:

  • Central Dogma Operations: DNA Transcription, Reverse Complement, Translation (DNA, RNA, REVC, PROT)
  • GC Content & OriC Finding: GC-Ratio skew curves, minimum skew origin detection (GC, BA1E, BA1F)
  • Point Mutations & Distances: Hamming distance $d_H$, Transition/Transversion ratio Ti/Tv (HAMM, TRAN)
  • Sequencing File Quality: FASTQ Phred quality scoring, adapter trimming (PHRE, FASTQ, BAP)

LEVEL 2 DYNAMIC PROGRAMMING & GRAPHS

🟡 Intermediate Tier

196 Solved Problems

Pairwise sequence alignment DP state machines, linear-space Hirschberg algorithms, De Bruijn graph Eulerian genome assembly, regulatory motif finding, and distance-based phylogenetic trees.

Key Curricular Milestones & Paradigms:

  • Pairwise Dynamic Programming: Global (Needleman-Wunsch), Local (Smith-Waterman), Affine Gotoh (GLOB, LOCA, GAFF)
  • Linear-Space Alignment: Hirschberg middle-edge divide-and-conquer in $O(n+m)$ space (BA5K, BA5L, EDTA)
  • De Bruijn Genome Assembly: Eulerian path/cycle traversals, paired-end $(k,d)$-mer reconstruction (BA3A-BA3M)
  • Regulatory Motif Discovery: Median String, Gibbs Sampling, Greedy Profile Search (BA2A-BA2H)
  • Evolutionary Trees: Additive phylogeny, UPGMA ultrametric trees, Neighbor-Joining (BA7A-BA7D)

LEVEL 3 STOCHASTICS, STRINGS & SPECTROMETRY

🔴 Advanced Tier

37 Solved Problems

Stochastic models (HMM Viterbi & Baum-Welch EM), Tandem Mass Spectrometry de novo peptide sequencing, Full-text indexing (Suffix Trees, BWT, FM-Index), Synteny 2-break rearrangements, and RNA secondary structure combinatorics.

Key Curricular Milestones & Paradigms:

  • Hidden Markov Models: Forward-Backward evaluation, Viterbi decoding, Baum-Welch parameter learning (BA10A-BA10J)
  • Mass Spectrometry & Proteomics: Branch-and-bound cyclopeptide sequencing, spectral convolution, PSM dictionaries (BA4A-BA4L, BA11A-BA11J)
  • Genome Rearrangements: Signed permutations, Hannenhalli-Pevzner theorem, 2-break distance $d = b - c$ (BA6A-BA6K, REVP)
  • Full-Text Indexing: Suffix Trees, Suffix Arrays, Burrows-Wheeler Transform, FM-Index backward search (BA9A-BA9R)
  • RNA Secondary Structures: Catalan and Motzkin non-crossing matchings, Nussinov DP folding (CAT, MOTZ, MMAT)
